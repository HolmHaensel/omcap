# Code of conduct

E-M1 Capture is a friendly community project for people who love their cameras, from beginners
to experts. We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to programming or photography.
- Assume good intent; criticise ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Respect privacy: do not post other people's photos or personal data.

Problems can be reported privately to the maintainer through GitHub (profile of
[HolmHaensel](https://github.com/HolmHaensel)). The maintainer may remove content and block
accounts that break these rules.
